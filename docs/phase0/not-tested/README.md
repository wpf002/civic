# Archived but not in the test set

Seven of the twelve 2025 Dallas council candidates whose sites were fetched, but whose pages
turned out to be accomplishments lists, biographies or donation pages rather than statements of
position.

They are kept because the ratio is itself a Phase 0 finding: **12 of 12 candidates had a website,
but only 1 of 12 (Chad West) had a page with substantial policy language.** The roadmap's other
kill criterion is that at least 40% of local candidates have findable stated positions. On this
sample, "has a website" and "has stated positions" are very different numbers.

Counting only pages with any first-person policy commitment, the five in the test set are the
usable ones, and even among those two are thin.
