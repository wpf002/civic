Re-Elect
Deputy Mayor Pro Tem Adam Bazaldua
Proven Leadership. Real Results. A Stronger Dallas.
For the past six years, Deputy Mayor Pro Tem Adam Bazaldua has been a fighter for District 7—championing safer neighborhoods, stronger local businesses, and a more accountable, effective city government. Now, as he runs for his final term on the Dallas City Council, Adam is ready to finish the job and continue delivering real results for our community.

Delivering for District 7 & Beyond
Safer Streets & Stronger Neighborhoods – Investing in infrastructure, reducing crime, and expanding community-based safety initiatives
Tackling Homelessness & Housing Challenges – Advocating for long-term solutions that provide real pathways to stability
Smart Economic Growth – Attracting local businesses, supporting small entrepreneurs, and ensuring development benefits our residents
Investing in Infrastructure & Sustainability – Improving roads, bike lanes, and public transit, and pushing for a cleaner, greener Dallas
Putting People First – Fighting for working families, standing up for reproductive rights, and ensuring every voice in our community is heard
